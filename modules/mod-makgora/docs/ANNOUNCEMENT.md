## Full (forum / wiki)

### Mak'Gora — leadership through ritual (AzerothCore)

For the last few weeks I have been building a new interpretation of **Mak'Gora** for AzerothCore, inspired by Classic Hardcore — not as a gimmick duel mode, but as **endgame progression** that permanently shapes the realm and creates lasting player stories.

Mak'Gora sits at the intersection of duel combat and hardcore stakes. Before the challenge, you walk a quest path that proves you are worthy. To claim a vacant seat you must defeat an **elite champion** (C++ scripted). When you enter the ritual you may accept **permadeath**: lose, and the ancestors keep your name; win, and you may take leadership of the Horde or the Alliance.

The module is aimed at **Classic Plus** realms with a level-60 cap and horizontal progression.

#### Highlights

- Quest chain that gates the right to challenge  
- One active player-leader per faction, with its own progression track  
- Elite champion encounter before coronation  
- Ceremony and appointment events with Horde / Alliance NPCs  
- Dynamic world reaction based on who holds the title  
- Afterlife content when a bound Mak'Gora ends in death  
- Arena spectators and cinematic scripting hooks  
- Unique rewards and cosmetic progression  
- Companion addon for atmosphere and live ritual state  

#### Status

Core systems (leadership state, ritual arming, duel resolution, permadeath locks, GM tools, champion stub, addon protocol) are in development as `mod-makgora`. Content layers (quests, ceremony, afterlife zone, full UI) follow a structured feature plan.

Feedback and testers welcome — especially RP-PvP / Classic Plus operators.
